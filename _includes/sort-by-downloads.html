{%- comment -%} Sort projects by last-month PyPI downloads (site.data.pypi_stats), most first. Projects without a count (templates, bots, forks) go last, keeping their data-file order. Param: items (array of projects). Result: assigns `sorted_items` in the caller's scope. Keys look like "|<999 - position>|" so a plain string sort orders by downloads and breaks ties by original position. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sbd_keys = '' | split: '' -%} {%- for sbd_item in include.items -%} {%- assign sbd_downloads = site.data.pypi_stats.packages[sbd_item.name] | default: 0 -%} {%- assign sbd_position = 999 | minus: forloop.index0 -%} {%- capture sbd_key -%}{{ sbd_downloads | prepend: '0000000000' | slice: -10, 10 }}|{{ sbd_position | prepend: '000' | slice: -3, 3 }}|{{ sbd_item.name }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign sbd_keys = sbd_keys | push: sbd_key -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign sbd_keys = sbd_keys | sort | reverse -%} {%- assign sorted_items = '' | split: '' -%} {%- for sbd_key in sbd_keys -%} {%- assign sbd_name = sbd_key | split: '|' | last -%} {%- assign sbd_proj = include.items | where: 'name', sbd_name | first -%} {%- assign sorted_items = sorted_items | push: sbd_proj -%} {%- endfor -%}
